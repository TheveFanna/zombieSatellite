# zombieSatellite: video script

A shot list for a video of at most 3 minutes (180 s). English only: narration, on-screen text and captions. The owner records and uploads the video; this file is the script.

Arc: the story hook (LES-1, heard again in 2012), a real pass with the phone raised and pointed, the beacon sound and the Doppler readout, the QSL card, and the demo mode as the closing beat.

Red lines for every shot: say that the beacon sound is simulated, never present the app as hearing a real satellite, and attribute every historical reception to the named person. The app is a finding, pointing and Doppler aid, not a generic satellite tracker.

## Recording notes

- Film shot 4 and shot 5 during one real pass. Pick a pass with a maximum elevation of at least 30° from the picker before the day, and check the weather.
- If no real pass can be filmed, show the demo mode in shots 4 and 5 instead, and say in the narration that this is the demo mode. Do not present demo footage as a real pass.
- Record the audio of shot 5 from the phone, so the tone is the one the app plays.
- Keep the camera backdrop on in shot 4 only if the camera permission was granted; otherwise film the phone from outside and keep the sky view.
- Margin: the shots add up to 170 s, which leaves 10 s under the limit of 180 s.
