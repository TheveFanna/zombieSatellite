In December two thousand five, NASA lost contact with IMAGE. The spacecraft went silent.

More than twelve years later, amateur astronomer Scott Tilley found its signal again.

What if a phone could help you take the first steps into that world?

zombieSatellite helps radio hobbyists find a pass, point toward a satellite, and follow its Doppler shift.

Seven spacecraft connect the experience to documented history. Five offer playable sessions.

Public orbital data predicts the next pass over your location. Observer coordinates are hidden here.

Choose a spacecraft and open its session. Times are shown in coordinated universal time.

The camera view overlays the computed satellite direction. Switch spacecraft to explore the sky around you.

Direction cues and the sky view help you orient the phone, even when an object is off screen.

Demo mode accelerates a predicted pass to sixty times normal speed, with automatic pointing available.

The result shows pointing accuracy and time on target. Each completed pass produces a simulated Q S L card.

Cards record the satellite and session details, and explicitly distinguish simulation from real radio reception.
